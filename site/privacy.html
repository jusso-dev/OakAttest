Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to all personal information collected by Yuma IT Pty Ltd, ABN 62 684 389 839 (we, us or our) through OakAttest and these product pages. We are bound by the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles.

This policy covers personal information Yuma IT collects when it operates OakAttest, when you use these product or documentation pages, or when you write to us. It does not cover personal information that customers collect or hold inside their own OakAttest deployments. Those customers are responsible for their own privacy notices.

1. What information do we collect?

The kind of Personal Information that we collect from you will depend on how you use the product or these pages. The Personal Information which we collect and hold about you may include:

name, email address, organisation, role, authentication identifiers, tenant and workspace records, engagement, assessment, evidence files and metadata, findings, assessor notes, export records, audit logs (user ID, tenant or workspace ID, action, resource, timestamp, IP address and user agent where available), and support correspondence. When you visit these pages we or the host typically hold the page requested, date and time, your IP address, and your browser user agent.

Customer evidence content is processed only to support the workflows selected by the customer or assessor firm.

2. Types of information

The Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) (Privacy Act) defines types of information, including Personal Information and Sensitive Information.

Personal Information means information or an opinion about an identified individual or an individual who is reasonably identifiable:

  1. whether the information or opinion is true or not; and
  2. whether the information or opinion is recorded in a material form or not.

If the information does not disclose your identity or enable your identity to be ascertained, it will in most cases not be classified as "Personal Information" and will not be subject to this privacy policy.

Sensitive Information is defined in the Privacy Act as including information or opinion about such things as an individual's racial or ethnic origin, political opinions, membership of a political association, religious or philosophical beliefs, membership of a trade union or other professional body, criminal record or health information.

We do not ask you to provide Sensitive Information as a condition of using OakAttest. Evidence or notes that a customer or assessor uploads may already contain it. Sensitive Information will be used by us only:

  1. for the primary purpose for which it was obtained;
  2. for a secondary purpose that is directly related to the primary purpose; and
  3. with your consent or where required or authorised by law.

3. How we collect your Personal Information

  1. We may collect Personal Information from you whenever you input such information into OakAttest, these pages, or provide it to us in any other way.
  2. We may also collect cookies from your computer which enable us to tell when you use the product and to keep you signed in. Those cookies are necessary for signed-in features. These marketing pages do not set first-party advertising or analytics cookies. Google Fonts may set cookies on Google's own domain when the typeface files are requested.
  3. We generally don't collect Sensitive Information, but when we do, we will comply with the preceding paragraph.
  4. Where reasonable and practicable we collect your Personal Information from you only. However, sometimes we may be given information from a third party (for example a tenant administrator who invites you). In cases like this we will take steps to make you aware of the information that was provided by a third party.

4. Purpose of collection

  1. We collect Personal Information to operate OakAttest, provide support, secure the service, maintain auditability, produce exports you request, and meet our legal, accounting and record-keeping obligations.
  2. We customarily only disclose Personal Information to service providers who assist us in operating the product. Final subprocessors, regions, and support tooling must be listed before public submission. Expected categories include Azure hosting, email delivery, logging or monitoring, and support tooling. We will confirm the current list in writing on request. These pages load fonts from Google Fonts. Your Personal Information may also be exposed from time to time to maintenance and support personnel acting in the normal course of their duties.
  3. If you have asked us about OakAttest, we may contact you about that request and about closely related services. We do not use Sensitive Information in direct marketing. Our messages will include a simple means by which you can request not to receive further communications of this nature. You can also opt out by emailing hello@yumait.com.au.

5. Security, Access and correction

  1. We store your Personal Information in a way that reasonably protects it from unauthorised access, misuse, modification or disclosure. Those steps include tenant scoping, role-based access controls, audit logging, private evidence storage, encryption in transit, and controlled downloads. When we no longer require your Personal Information for the purpose for which we obtained it, we will take reasonable steps to destroy, anonymise or de-identify it. Retention of product records is governed by the customer's tenant settings, assessment retention settings, support obligations, legal hold, and applicable law. Most of the Personal Information that is stored in our client files and records will be kept for a maximum of 7 years to fulfil our record keeping obligations.
  2. The Australian Privacy Principles:
    1. permit you to obtain access to the Personal Information we hold about you in certain circumstances (Australian Privacy Principle 12); and
    2. allow you to correct inaccurate Personal Information subject to certain exceptions (Australian Privacy Principle 13).
  3. Where you would like to obtain such access, or ask us to correct or delete information we are not required to keep, please contact us in writing on the contact details set out at the bottom of this privacy policy. There is no charge to make a request. We will respond within 30 days. We may ask you to verify your identity. If we refuse a request we will tell you why in writing.
  4. If an eligible data breach occurs (unauthorised access, disclosure or loss of personal information that is likely to result in serious harm), we will notify affected individuals and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner as soon as practicable, as required by the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme. We do not notify every security incident.

6. Complaint procedure

If you have a complaint concerning the manner in which we maintain the privacy of your Personal Information, please contact us on the contact details set out at the bottom of this policy. We will acknowledge your complaint within five business days and give you a written response within 30 days. We may seek further information from you to clarify your concerns. If we agree that your complaint is well founded, we will, in consultation with you, take appropriate steps to rectify the problem. If you remain dissatisfied with the outcome, you may refer the matter to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/privacy-complaints or by phone on 1300 363 992.

7. Overseas transfer

OakAttest SaaS is intended to run in publisher-controlled Azure infrastructure. Final subprocessors and regions must be listed before public submission. We will confirm the current position in writing on request. We will not name a country here that we have not confirmed. Google Fonts is provided by Google LLC, a United States company. A request for the typeface files may be handled outside Australia.

Where Personal Information is handled outside Australia we take reasonable steps to ensure the recipient handles it consistently with the Australian Privacy Principles. We remain accountable for that disclosure.

8. How to contact us about privacy

If you have any queries, or if you seek access to your Personal Information, or if you have a complaint about our privacy practices, you can contact us through: hello@yumait.com.au.
