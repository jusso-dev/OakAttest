Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to all personal information collected by Yuma IT Pty Ltd, ABN 62 684 389 839 (we, us or our) through OakAttest and these documentation pages. We are bound by the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles.

This policy does not cover personal information that customers collect or hold inside their own OakAttest deployments. Those customers are responsible for their own privacy notices.

1. What information do we collect?

The kind of Personal Information that we collect from you will depend on how you use the product or these pages. The Personal Information which we collect and hold about you may include:

name, email address, organisation, role, authentication identifiers, tenant and workspace records, engagement, assessment, evidence files and metadata, findings, assessor notes, export records, audit logs (user ID, tenant or workspace ID, action, resource, timestamp, IP address and user agent where available), and support correspondence. When you visit these pages we or the host typically hold the page requested, date and time, your IP address, and your browser user agent.

2. Types of information

The Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) (Privacy Act) defines types of information, including Personal Information and Sensitive Information.

Personal Information means information or an opinion about an identified individual or an individual who is reasonably identifiable, whether the information or opinion is true or not, and whether it is recorded in a material form or not.

Sensitive Information includes information or opinion about an individual's racial or ethnic origin, political opinions, membership of a political association, religious or philosophical beliefs, membership of a trade union or other professional body, criminal record or health information.

We do not ask you to provide Sensitive Information as a condition of using OakAttest. Evidence or notes that a customer or assessor uploads may already contain it. Sensitive Information will be used by us only for the primary purpose for which it was obtained, for a directly related secondary purpose, or with your consent or where required or authorised by law.

3. How we collect your Personal Information

We may collect Personal Information from you whenever you input it into OakAttest, these pages, or provide it to us in any other way. We may collect cookies that keep you signed in. We generally collect Personal Information from you only, but sometimes a third party such as a tenant administrator may give it to us.

4. Purpose of collection

We collect Personal Information to operate OakAttest, provide support, secure the service, maintain auditability, produce exports you request, and meet our legal, accounting and record-keeping obligations. Final subprocessors, regions, and support tooling must be listed before public submission. Expected categories include Azure hosting, email delivery, logging or monitoring, and support tooling. We will confirm the current list in writing on request. If you have asked us about OakAttest, we may contact you about that request and about closely related services. You can opt out by emailing hello@yumait.com.au.

5. Security, Access and correction

We store your Personal Information in a way that reasonably protects it from unauthorised access, misuse, modification or disclosure. Those steps include tenant scoping, role-based access controls, audit logging, private evidence storage, encryption in transit, and controlled downloads. Retention of product records is governed by the customer's tenant settings, assessment retention settings, support obligations, legal hold, and applicable law.

You may ask for access to the Personal Information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete information we are not required to keep, by writing to the contact details at the bottom of this policy. There is no charge. We will respond within 30 days.

If an eligible data breach occurs (unauthorised access, disclosure or loss of personal information that is likely to result in serious harm), we will notify affected individuals and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner as soon as practicable, as required by the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme. We do not notify every security incident.

6. Complaint procedure

If you have a complaint concerning the manner in which we maintain the privacy of your Personal Information, please contact us on the contact details set out at the bottom of this policy. We will acknowledge your complaint within five business days and give you a written response within 30 days. If you remain dissatisfied, you may refer the matter to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/privacy-complaints or by phone on 1300 363 992.

7. Overseas transfer

OakAttest SaaS is intended to run in publisher-controlled Azure infrastructure. Final subprocessors and regions must be listed before public submission. We will confirm the current position in writing on request. We will not name a country here that we have not confirmed. Where Personal Information is handled outside Australia we take reasonable steps to ensure the recipient handles it consistently with the Australian Privacy Principles. We remain accountable for that disclosure.

8. How to contact us about privacy

If you have any queries, or if you seek access to your Personal Information, or if you have a complaint about our privacy practices, you can contact us through: hello@yumait.com.au.
